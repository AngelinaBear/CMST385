Booking

Ready to get rolling? Complete the form below to request a group class spot or schedule a private lesson. Please provide as much detail as possible so I can match you with the best session for your goals and experience level. Submitting this form does not guarantee a booking. You will receive a confirmation email once your session has been reviewed and approved.

Group classes follow the weekly schedule listed on the Session Info page. If you are requesting a group class, please select the appropriate category and preferred date. Private lessons are scheduled separately based on availability. After submitting your request, I will follow up to confirm a time and provide next steps.

Group classes are held at a local indoor skating rink in Stafford. Skaters may bring their own skates or rent from the rink for an additional fee. Please arrive 15 minutes early to allow time for check-in, skate adjustments, and warm-up.

Group classes are $25 per skater for a 60-minute session. Private lessons are $50 for a 60-minute session. Payment details will be provided in your confirmation email.

Cancellations must be made at least 24 hours in advance to receive credit toward a future session. Late cancellations or no-shows may result in forfeited payment.
