About Me

Hi there! I’m Trinity, a roller-skating instructor who believes skating should feel empowering, not intimidating. What started as a personal passion quickly grew into something much bigger, and now I teach both group and private lessons for skaters of all ages and skill levels. From complete beginners learning to stand comfortably on skates to experienced riders refining transitions and flow, my goal is to help every student build strong fundamentals and real confidence on wheels.

I create a supportive environment where mistakes are part of the learning process and progress is celebrated at every stage. My lessons focus on balance, technique, and control, but most importantly, helping you feel confident and free when you skate. Skating transformed the way I move and express myself, and I love guiding others as they discover that same freedom.

How to Start Skating Backwards with Confidence

Skating backwards can feel intimidating at first, but it is one of the most rewarding skills to learn. Once you understand the fundamentals, it becomes smooth, controlled, and even relaxing. Here is how to start building confidence while rolling in reverse.

1. Start with a Stable Stance
Before moving, bend your knees and keep your feet about shoulder-width apart. Stay low and relaxed. A slight bend in your knees gives you balance and control.

2. Use the “C-Cut” Motion
Instead of pushing straight back, make small “C” shapes with your feet.
